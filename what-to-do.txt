## HomeView: On Repeat / Most Played Accuracy

The "On Repeat" carousel (and most-played ranking generally) relies only on Navidrome's
play_count field, which only reflects plays that were scrobbled back to the server.
Local scrobbles queued in scrobble_queue (before flush) and any plays that happened
offline or failed to scrobble are not counted.

Should blend both sources: Navidrome play_count + local scrobble count from the
scrobble_queue or a dedicated local_play_count column in the tracks/albums table.
The local count should be authoritative during a session and reconciled on flush.

### Files likely involved
  - src/hooks/useListeningStats.ts — builds the ranked stats query
  - src/db/migrations.ts — may need a local_play_count column or derived view
  - src/lib/navidrome.ts — sync step that writes play_count from server

## HomeView: Suggestion Grid Visual Polish

The suggestion grid at the top of HomeView has good bones but looks bad visually.
Needs a proper design pass from a better model.

### What it is
Horizontal row of "smart suggestion" cards filling the full width of the home screen.
Each card is a context-aware album pick with a label chip:
  - "Jump back in" (blue) — most recently played
  - "Rediscover" (amber) — most played but not heard recently
  - "Long time no hear" (violet) — oldest last-scrobbled album from SQLite
  - "New to your library" (green) — recently added, not in recent plays
  - "More from [Artist]" (rose) — only appears while a track is playing

### What's wrong
Looks rough — likely issues with:
  - Sizing/proportions of the album art thumbnail vs card height
  - Blurred background not looking polished enough
  - Text/chip styling not cohesive
  - Gap/spacing between cards

### Files to fix
  - src/components/HomeView.tsx — SuggestionGrid + SuggestionCard components (around line 176)
  - src/styles/home.css — .suggestion-grid, .suggestion-card, .suggestion-card__* rules
