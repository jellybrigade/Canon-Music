## HomeView: On Repeat / Most Played Accuracy

The "On Repeat" carousel (and most-played ranking generally) relies only on Navidrome's
play_count field, which only reflects plays that were scrobbled back to the server.
Local scrobbles queued in scrobble_queue (before flush) and any plays that happened
offline or failed to scrobble are not counted.

Should blend both sources: Navidrome play_count + local scrobble count from the
scrobble_queue or a dedicated local_play_count column in the tracks/albums table.
The local count should be authoritative during a session and reconciled on flush.

### Files likely involved
  - src/hooks/useListeningStats.ts — builds the ranked stats query
  - src/db/migrations.ts — may need a local_play_count column or derived view
  - src/lib/navidrome.ts — sync step that writes play_count from server
