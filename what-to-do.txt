## Radio: Curated Radio Lacks Variety

Curated Radio over-concentrates on a single artist and album. Example: seeding with
"XS" by Rina Sawayama produced 9 consecutive tracks from Jessie Ware, all from the
same album. Root cause likely in the candidate scoring / deduplication step — no
per-artist or per-album cap enforced on the lookahead queue.

Should enforce hard limits: e.g. max 3 tracks per artist and 2 tracks per album within
any 10-track lookahead window.

### Files likely involved
  - src/hooks/useRadio.ts or wherever the radio queue is built
  - src/lib/radio.ts or the scoring/candidate selection logic

## Bug: Right-Click on Album in Home View Shows Browser Context Menu

Right-clicking an album cover in Home view triggers the browser's native image context
menu ("Open image in new tab", "Save image as…") instead of the app's album context
menu. The `onContextMenu` handler is either missing or being swallowed by the `<img>`
element before it bubbles up.

### Files likely involved
  - src/components/HomeView.tsx or the album card component it renders
  - Wherever the album context menu is defined/triggered

## HomeView: On Repeat / Most Played Accuracy

The "On Repeat" carousel (and most-played ranking generally) relies only on Navidrome's
play_count field, which only reflects plays that were scrobbled back to the server.
Local scrobbles queued in scrobble_queue (before flush) and any plays that happened
offline or failed to scrobble are not counted.

Should blend both sources: Navidrome play_count + local scrobble count from the
scrobble_queue or a dedicated local_play_count column in the tracks/albums table.
The local count should be authoritative during a session and reconciled on flush.

### Files likely involved
  - src/hooks/useListeningStats.ts — builds the ranked stats query
  - src/db/migrations.ts — may need a local_play_count column or derived view
  - src/lib/navidrome.ts — sync step that writes play_count from server
